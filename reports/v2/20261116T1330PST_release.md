**How a number is tied (a design choice of this session, stated in the file).**
- An image number is tied by its cell (tile and quantity) and then checked by value.
- A text number is tied by value, at its printed precision, among candidates whose context cue is in its sentence. The
  candidates are record values, statistics derived from them as the text states them (e.g. the AP median of AD K/K₀),
  and fixed setup values. Without the cue, every small integer matched some value.

**Updates that matter most:**

  GM1's AD optimum and the GM1, GM3 and GM4 A_crit.

  - AP AD K/K₀ median 21 → 9.0;
  - GM AD K/K₀ maximum 20 → 3.1;
