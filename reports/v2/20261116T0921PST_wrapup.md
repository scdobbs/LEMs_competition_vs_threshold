**The rule (2.3; F3).** At S_c = 1.25 the fit holds on 5 of 5 tiles and D on 0 of 5 (D/D₀ 1.7-2.7). At S_c = 0.9 the
fit holds on 4 of 5 (GM3 fails at 1.058) and D on 2 of 5 (GM3, GM4); both hold on GM4 only. **The flux-form model is not
preferred on GM**, by either reading of "at some S_c ≥ 0.9" (one S_c shared by the tiles: 0 and 1 of 5 tiles; each tile
at either S_c: 1 of 5, GM4). Which condition held where:
- **Fit only:** at 1.25 on all five tiles; at 0.9 on GM1, GM2, GM5.
- **D only:** at 0.9 on GM3; at the fitted S_c on GM1 (D/D₀ 0.79 at MSE × 1.05-1.06).
- **Both:** GM4 at 0.9; GM2 at its fitted S_c (0.540 and 0.565; MSE × 0.87, D/D₀ 1.40). By 2.3 the fitted S_c (< 0.9)
  can support "closer D" (GM1, GM2) but not "preferred".
- At the fitted S_c on GM3, GM4 and GM5 every evaluation is `no_steady_solution`: there is no steady flux-form surface
  with fewer than 1 % of interior hillslope cells at the clip, at any (A_crit, D) of the grid.

**Two features of the numbers.**
- **D falls as S_c falls**, on every tile where the profile exists: GM1 1.69e-2 (1.25, 0.9) to 7.1e-3 (0.54); GM2 2.6e-2,
  2.1e-2, 1.4e-2; GM3 1.7e-2, 1.4e-2; GM4 1.7e-2, 1.4e-2; GM5 1.7e-2 at both. With the flux form the fitted D does move
  toward D₀ as the nonlinearity engages (the fraction of hillslope cells above 0.5 S_c at the optimum is 1-12 % at 1.25,
  13-45 % at 0.9, 70-81 % at the fitted S_c), unlike the centre-form Phase 6 joint, which returned the linear D.
- **The flux form often fits better than the linear joint at the same K**: MSE × 0.91-0.99 on GM1, GM2 and GM5 at 0.9,
  × 0.87-0.88 on GM2 at its fitted S_c, and × 0.99 on GM2, GM4 and GM5 at 1.25. This is at fixed K and on a coarse grid, so it is a bound on the
  improvement, not a measured one.

**Where the grid limits the answer.** The optimum sits on the upper A_crit edge of the grid on GM1 (0.9, fitted, p84) and
GM2 (fitted, p84): the grid spans the linear joint 5 % interval × 2, and the nonlinear optimum moves to larger A_crit as
S_c falls. The 1 % contour is one grid point in 12 of the 14 profiles that have an optimum (D step × 1.24, A_crit step about × 1.4), so D is resolved
only to that step. Neither changes the verdict at S_c = 1.25, where the D condition fails by a factor ≥ 1.1 beyond the
1.5 bound on every tile; at 0.9 a finer D grid could move GM1 and GM5 (1.88, 2.23) by at most one step (× 1.24), which
does not reach 1.5.

What the numbers show, beside the rule:
- The partitioned surfaces get the hillslope gradients right (median and p90 within × 1.25 on nearly every tile and
  surface) and the crests wrong: their crest curvature is 2-8 × too low and their E*-R* ratio 1.5-5 × too high, i.e. they
  are the near-parabolic hillslopes of an effective linear D. AD gets the crests 2-22 × too sharp.
- Lowering S_c in the flux form moves both crest measures toward the observed values on every GM tile (crest curvature
  0.40-0.58 at 1.25 → 0.43-0.69 at 0.9 → 0.72-0.96 at the fitted S_c; E*-R* ratio 1.5-2.0 → 1.3-1.8 → 0.91-1.02). At the
  fitted S_c on GM1 three of the four measures pass and the p90 gradient fails at 0.780 (0.796 at the p84 S_c; limit 0.800); on GM2 the crest
  curvature (0.72) and the p90 gradient (0.77) fail. The cost is the steep tail of the gradients, which the clip at
  0.99 S_c caps: with S_c ≈ 0.54-0.56 the model cannot produce the 10 % of hillslope cells steeper than about 0.55.
- So the morphology score answers addendum 9's reason 2 in part: the MSE does not reward hillslope form (surfaces with
  MSE within 2-6 % of each other differ by × 2 in crest curvature), and the one model class that approaches the form, the
  flux form at a low S_c, exists as a steady surface on only 2 of 5 GM tiles.

## 7. What the evidence supports, and where it is weak

**Supported.**
1. **The flux-form nonlinear model is not preferred on GM** under the pre-registered rule, on either reading. At S_c 1.25
   it fits as well as the linear joint (MSE within 3 % on 5 of 5 tiles) and leaves D where the linear joint has it
   (1.7-2.7 × D₀); at 0.9 both conditions hold on GM4 only.
2. **With the flux form, D moves toward D₀ as S_c falls, at no or little cost in fit, until the steady solution
   disappears.** At the fitted path-scale S_c the gap closes on GM1 (D/D₀ 0.79, MSE × 1.06) and GM2 (1.40, × 0.87), and
   there is no steady surface at all on GM3, GM4 and GM5. This is the first nonlinear run in the project in which D
   responds to the nonlinearity; the centre-form Phase 6 joint did not.
3. **No fitted surface reproduces the observed hillslope form** (0 of 5 tiles per site for every surface). The linear and
   centre-form surfaces have the right gradients and crests 2-8 × too blunt; AD has crests 2-22 × too sharp. The flux
   form at the fitted S_c comes closest (GM1 fails only the p90 gradient, by 2-3 %), so reason 2 of addendum 9 ("the MSE
   does not reward hillslope form") is supported: surfaces within a few per cent in MSE differ by × 2 in crest curvature.
4. **Test 2:** on the accepted DT2 head sets the fitted A_crit bracket overlaps the head-area IQR on 5 of 5 tiles at both
   sites, and an inverse area-slope relation is present at both sites by the branch's rule. On GM it is robust (r² 0.54;
   every variant excludes 0).
5. **CI** fails for identified reasons that are fixed, and a push run is green on 3.11 and 3.12.

**Weak.**
1. **The flux-form answer rests on a coarse grid at fixed K.** D steps × 1.24 and A_crit × 1.4; the 1 % contour is one
   grid point in 12 of 14 profiles; four optima sit on the upper A_crit edge of the grid (GM1 at 0.9 and the fitted S_c,
   GM2 at the fitted S_c). K was not refitted, so the "fit" condition compares surfaces at the linear joint K.
2. **The fitted S_c is a path-scale quantity** (S_c ≈ S̄/0.8, addendum 8), and the clip acts on 1 m cell gradients
   (addendum 9 E5). Where a GM tile has no steady solution at its fitted S_c, that is the same scale mismatch, not
   necessarily a property of the landscape. The 1.4 rule (excluding cells next to fixed cells) changed the outcome
   counts on 4 of 20 profiles (e.g. GM1 at 0.544: 140 converged against 124 by the addendum 9 rule) and no optimum: the
   best (A_crit, D) and its MSE are the same under both rules on all four.
3. **Test 2's AP relation is fragile** (r² 0.09; lost with D-infinity areas, the centred 10 m slope or the Wiener-only
   set), the IQR-overlap rule is easy to pass when head areas span two orders of magnitude, a quarter to a third of the
   heads drain less than 100 m², and Method D never ran, so the method-agreement criterion is untested.
4. **CI: an intermittent 3.11 test failure** (run 43521225354) on code that later passed; its test is not identified.
5. **The corrections list does not exist yet** (no .docx). And AP nonlinear work stays stopped (1.2), so nothing here
   tests a nonlinear model on AP.
